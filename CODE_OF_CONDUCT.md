# Community guidelines

AgentQit welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds
and levels of experience.

- Discuss ideas and technical evidence constructively.
- Be patient with questions and acknowledge mistakes without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or share another person's private information.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project.
- Use synthetic data in examples and respect the security reporting policy.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close discussions, and restrict
participation when necessary. Repeated or severe abuse can result in a permanent ban.

Use GitHub's private reporting tools to report abusive content or account behavior.
For a security vulnerability in AgentQit, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
